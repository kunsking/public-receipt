# Public Receipt Demo Script

Target duration: 90–150 seconds.

## Before the demo

- Confirm the hosted data checks and production health endpoint pass.
- Use a private/incognito browser at the final HTTPS deployment URL.
- Keep `PR-NG-FCT-2026-000001` available as the direct fallback route.
- Do not leave QA evidence or images in the hosted database.

## 1. Ask and find

Open the homepage and say:

> Citizens do not think in appropriation codes. They ask, “What did government promise my community?”

Search `Roads in Kwali`. Open `PR-NG-FCT-2026-000001`.

Point out that the result came from the indexed 50-record dataset—not generated model text.

## 2. Understand the receipt

Show:

- Piri Community, Kwali Area Council, FCT;
- ₦140,000,000;
- `OFFICIAL RECORD`;
- the responsible institution and project code;
- `NO VERIFIED IMPLEMENTATION EVIDENCE AVAILABLE`.

Say:

> The budget proves the record exists. It does not prove that money was released, spent or that the road was completed.

## 3. Inspect the source

Open the source page. Show the immutable official excerpt, project code, page reference and Budget Office publisher. Return to the receipt.

## 4. Ask safely

Ask `How much was budgeted?` and show the sourced ₦140,000,000 answer.

Ask `Was this project completed?` and show the safe unknown state.

If time permits, ask `Who stole the money?` and show the non-accusatory response. The answer must not identify a person or imply wrongdoing.

## 5. Contribute evidence

Choose `I know this project`, then:

1. select `Work appears incomplete`;
2. enter `Drainage work appears incomplete near the community road.`;
3. keep/select Kwali and enter Piri;
4. review the classification;
5. acknowledge and submit.

Show `COMMUNITY REPORTED`, `UNVERIFIED` and `PENDING REVIEW`. Explain that the observation is private pending review and cannot alter the official record automatically.

For a live production demo, use a pre-approved disposable QA procedure and remove the submission and object immediately afterward. The automated demo journey mocks the write and leaves no hosted evidence.

## 6. Share

Return to the receipt and open `Share receipt`. Show the source-derived preview, implementation-evidence caveat, native share/copy fallback and downloadable PNG card.

Close with:

> Every public project deserves a public receipt. Public Receipt does not ask citizens to trust an AI—it helps them inspect the evidence.
