# Public Receipt Trust Model

Public Receipt keeps source claims and community evidence visibly separate. The canonical vocabulary is:

1. **Official Record** — a claim transcribed from a retained government source. This proves the source contains the record, not that the project was funded or delivered.
2. **Verified Evidence** — evidence whose provenance and relationship to the project have been checked through the controlled verification flow.
3. **Corroborated Community Evidence** — community evidence supported by more than one credible, independent observation, but not elevated to an official record.
4. **Community Report** — a submitted observation awaiting verification.
5. **Unverified** — material that has not passed the applicable verification checks.
6. **Disputed** — a record or evidence item with a documented, material conflict that remains unresolved.

These labels describe evidence state. They are not political scores, delivery ratings or performance labels.

## Core rules

**No source, no claim.** Every public civic record must retain its source document, URL, excerpt and location within the source.

**Budgeted ≠ Released ≠ Spent ≠ Completed.** A budget line supports only the statement that an appropriation record exists. Release, expenditure and physical completion each require their own evidence.

Browser clients have read-only access to public civic records. Community evidence submission and verification remain server-controlled and are not enabled in M1.
